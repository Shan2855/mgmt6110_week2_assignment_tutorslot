## 2. My findings

### Finding 1
- Where: https://mgmt6110week2assignmenttutorslot.vercel.app/ — Chat Request form from a tutor profile.
- What I did, what I saw: I opened Chat Request, entered a name, entered a non-SMU/invalid email address, added a message, and clicked Send Request. TutorSlot accepted the request and displayed "Chat Request Sent!" even though the form asks for an SMU email.
- Which heuristic: 5 — Error Prevention.
- Screen or system: Screen, because the Chat Request form could validate the email before submission and prevent an invalid or non-SMU email from being accepted.
- Severity, and why: 3 — Major usability problem because incorrect student contact information can be submitted successfully, which could interfere with identification or communication with the student.
- The repair: The form should validate the email format and require an approved SMU email domain before allowing the request to be submitted, with a clear message explaining the required format.

### Finding 2
- Where: https://mgmt6110week2assignmenttutorslot.vercel.app/ — Find a Tutor, Faculty Info, and My Week.
- What I did, what I saw: I used TutorSlot as a returning student and looked for a shortcut to a tutor or subject I had previously booked. I found no saved preferences, favourite tutors, recently booked tutors/classes, or quick "Book Again" option.
- Which heuristic: 7 — Flexibility and Efficiency of Use.
- Screen or system: System, because remembering previous tutors, classes, or preferences requires booking-history or preference data across visits rather than a change to one screen alone.
- Severity, and why: 2 — Minor usability problem because the normal booking flow still works, but frequent users may have to repeatedly find the same tutor or subject.
- The repair: Returning students should have shortcuts such as Recent Tutors, Favourites, Saved Preferences, or Book Again based on their previous bookings.

### Finding 3
- Where: https://mgmt6110week2assignmenttutorslot.vercel.app/ — In-Person Tutoring Weather Outlook.
- What I did, what I saw: I checked the weather information while considering tutoring sessions that could be booked several days ahead. TutorSlot displayed only the official NEA 2-hour weather outlook, so the weather information did not help with planning travel for sessions later in the week.
- Which heuristic: 7 — Flexibility and Efficiency of Use.
- Screen or system: System, because providing weather for future session dates requires forecast data beyond the current 2-hour weather response.
- Severity, and why: 2 — Minor usability problem because booking still works normally, but the weather feature is less useful when planning travel for future tutoring sessions.
- The repair: TutorSlot should provide a multi-day weather forecast, ideally matching the dates of tutoring sessions available for booking.

### Finding 4
- Where: https://mgmt6110week2assignmenttutorslot.vercel.app/ — during an in-progress TutorSlot task.
- What I did, what I saw: While using TutorSlot, I pressed the browser Back button expecting to return to the previous state in the application. Instead, the browser left TutorSlot and returned to the page I had visited before the application.
- Which heuristic: 3 — User Control and Freedom.
- Screen or system: System, because browser history and in-app navigation state need to be handled across the application rather than corrected on one individual screen.
- Severity, and why: 2 — Minor usability problem because it can unexpectedly interrupt a user's task, although the user can reopen TutorSlot and continue.
- The repair: Browser Back should return the user to an appropriate previous state within TutorSlot during an in-app workflow instead of unexpectedly taking the user out of the application.

### Finding 5
- Where: https://mgmt6110week2assignmenttutorslot.vercel.app/ — My Week and booking/payment information.
- What I did, what I saw: I reviewed the date information after booking sessions and saw labels such as "Thursday, Thu, Sep 10", where the weekday was displayed twice.
- Which heuristic: 8 — Aesthetic and Minimalist Design.
- Screen or system: Screen, because the displayed date text can be simplified without requiring additional system data or functionality.
- Severity, and why: 1 — Cosmetic problem because the duplicated weekday does not prevent the user from understanding or completing the task.
- The repair: Display the weekday only once, for example "Thursday, Sep 10" or "Thu, Sep 10".
